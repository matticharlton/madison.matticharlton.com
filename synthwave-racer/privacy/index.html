Privacy policy

Synthwave Racer privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026. Publisher: Telephasic Workshop Ltd., Toronto, Canada.

Synthwave Racer does not collect personal data. We have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the game sends nothing to us.

Data we collectNone
Ads or trackingNone
Data sold or sharedNone

Stored only on your device

Your settings, best times, saved ghost laps, chosen craft, paint and driver, music choices and any music you import are saved on your device. They stay there and are deleted when you delete the app.

Hand and head tracking

On Apple Vision Pro, with your permission, the game reads your hand positions to steer and your head position to place the cockpit around you. visionOS provides this data while you play. The game uses it at that moment and does not store it, record it or send it anywhere.

Motion sensors on iPhone

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads your iPhone's motion sensors to turn the craft. It uses the reading at that moment and does not store it, record it or send it anywhere.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your lap times go to Game Center leaderboards. In online races, Game Center sends your racer's position, speed, craft and colour to the other players in that race. Other players see your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center under the Apple Privacy Policy, and we don't receive your Game Center account details.

Purchases

Apple handles all payments for Synthwave Racer Pro and for tips. We never see your payment details. The game checks your purchase with Apple on your device.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
